Everything in the course was written for Ulster (Donegal) Irish in Caighdeán spelling. These are the lines I'm least sure of. Tick them off, or tell me the fix and I'll update the text and regenerate the audio.

**Two things apply throughout:**
- The **sound-it-out** spellings are rough English-letter guides, written with Ulster pronunciation in mind. Trust the ABAIR audio over them.
- The **advisor notes** (literal meaning and "why") simplify the grammar on purpose. Flag any that are misleading.
